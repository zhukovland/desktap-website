# … the helpers from the docs: cell, post, json …
last="" sent_at=-100
while true; do
  free=$(df -g / | awk 'NR == 2 { print $4 }')
  # send a change at once, the same value every 30 s
  if [[ $free != $last ]] || (( SECONDS - sent_at >= 30 )); then
    title=$(json "$free GB free")
    post "{\"cellId\":\"$cell\",\"title\":$title}" &&
      last=$free sent_at=$SECONDS
  fi
  sleep 10
done

FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

What an AI can touch, what Desktap needs from your Mac, and what it costs. The rest is in the docs.

What can an AI do on my Mac without me?

Changes an AI sends through Desktap wait for your Accept on your iPhone or iPad. Through Desktap, on its own, it can look: your profiles, pages and buttons, your apps and which one is in front, whether your startup scripts run. It can also restart a startup script that is already on your deck.

A new or changed button, page or profile arrives on the phone as a card that shows every script it runs, and nothing changes until you tap Accept. Running a command on your Mac is off until you turn on Allow AI to Run Commands, and then you allow each command. Through Desktap, an AI can’t press your buttons or delete anything.

This covers what goes through Desktap. An AI app that runs commands by its own means, such as Claude Code in Terminal, follows that app’s permissions.

Do I need to write code?

No. Most buttons need none: pick a type such as Launch App, Key Combination, System Action, Run Shortcut or Text Snippet, and set it up on the phone. Ready-made live widgets wait in the Gallery, and an AI app can write the rest for you.

Scripts are there for when no type does the job: zsh or AppleScript, with recipes you can copy as they are.

Which AI apps work with it, and do I need one?

Claude Desktop, Claude Code and ChatGPT / Codex connect with Connect in the agent’s AI Assistants. Cursor, Windsurf and any other MCP client take a config you copy from the same place.

An AI app is optional. Everything it builds, you can also build by hand on the phone.

Does it work away from my home Wi-Fi?

Your iPhone or iPad and your Mac need to be on the same Wi-Fi network: at home, at the office, wherever both are. They talk to each other directly over an encrypted connection; there is no Desktap server to carry a tap over the internet.

You pair once, with a 6-digit code. After that, the phone finds the Mac and reconnects by itself.

Which macOS and iOS do I need?

A Mac with macOS 15 or later, and an iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later. Desktap Agent lives in the menu bar; it has no Dock icon.

Docs: What you need
Why does the agent ask for Accessibility?

macOS lets an app press keys and type for you only with Accessibility. Desktap Agent needs it for Key Combination, Text Snippet and most System Actions: the keys it presses and the text it types when you tap those buttons.

You can skip it in Setup. Those buttons then flash red with “Accessibility access needed”; Launch App, Open URL and scripts that don’t press keys work without it. You can turn it off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

Docs: macOS permissions
Is there a Windows or Android version?

No. Desktap is native to Apple devices: an agent for the Mac and an app for iPhone and iPad, which share your deck through iCloud. There is no Windows agent and no Android app.

Docs: What you need
Where is my data stored?

On your devices and in your own iCloud. Your deck, with its profiles, pages, buttons and their scripts, syncs between your iPhone and iPad through your iCloud account. What your scripts remember stays in a private folder on your Mac and is never synced.

There is no Desktap server, no account and no analytics. If you connect an AI app, what you ask it goes to that app’s servers, under its own privacy policy.

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

The Mac agent is free. The iPhone and iPad app is free with two profiles and two pages in each, with every button type, live widgets and AI apps included.

Desktap Pro unlocks unlimited profiles and pages and lets you reorder pages: monthly, yearly or a one-time Lifetime purchase, through the App Store. A profile or page past the free plan is never deleted: it stays locked until Pro opens it.
